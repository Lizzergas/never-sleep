Overview

Never Sleep is an Android utility that changes your device screen-off timeout when you explicitly enable the feature. Lizz Studio develops and provides the app. Contact us at home@lizz.com about this policy or your data.

Information we process

  • Screen timeout setting — When you turn Never Sleep on, the app writes a higher value to Android’s system screen-off timeout and stores your previous timeout locally so it can be restored.
  • App preferences — The previous timeout, current mode, and ads preference are stored in the app’s private storage. The app does not send these settings to Lizz Studio. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for the app’s stored data.
  • Advertising data — If you leave ads enabled and the applicable consent flow permits ad requests, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share your IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

What we do not do

  • The app has no user accounts and does not request names, emails, or contacts.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not upload your screen-timeout preference to our servers — it stays on your device. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address, to respond to your request.
  • We do not request your precise location, contacts, photos, or files.

Permissions

Modify system settings — Required for the core feature. You grant this manually in Android Settings → Apps → Special app access.

Internet & network state — Used to refresh the privacy choices required for Google advertising and, when banner ads are enabled, to load ads. You can disable ad requests in the in-app Settings menu.

Security and sharing

Android’s app sandbox protects the app’s local preferences. Google Mobile Ads encrypts the data it collects in transit using TLS. Advertising and consent data are processed by Google under its policies; the app does not send your screen-timeout setting to Google. Lizz Studio does not operate an analytics or account server for this app. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences remain until you clear the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall it. Turn Never Sleep off first so it can restore your previous timeout: clearing storage or uninstalling while it is enabled may leave the system timeout unchanged. You can also set your preferred timeout in Android’s display settings.

Google controls the retention of advertising and consent data it processes. See Google’s retention policy and privacy controls. Turning ads off stops new banner ad requests; it does not delete data already held by Google. Android’s privacy settings let you reset or delete your advertising ID.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to address your inquiry, meet legal obligations, or handle an ongoing dispute. You can request deletion by emailing home@lizz.com. We will explain any information we must retain. There is no in-app account to delete.

Your choices

You can turn ads off at any time in Never Sleep → Settings. Where required, Settings also includes Privacy choices so you can review or change the consent choices presented by Google. You can review or revoke Modify system settings access in Android Settings without uninstalling the app. Turn Never Sleep off before revoking access if you want it to restore your previous timeout. Where applicable, you may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have provided to us, or raise a concern with your local data protection authority.

Children

Never Sleep is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Any consent required for a new use of data will be requested separately.
