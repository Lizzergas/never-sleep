How to keep your Android screen on

Turn it on when you need it. Turn it off to restore your previous timeout.

  1. 01

    Allow access

    Grant “Modify system settings” so Never Sleep can change your screen timeout.

  2. 02

    Tap ON

    Your screen stays awake, even after you leave the app.

  3. 03

    Keep it within reach

    Edit your Quick Settings and add Never Sleep, or place its widget on your home screen.
